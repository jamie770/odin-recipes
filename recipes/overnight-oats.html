An overnight oats recipe for 2!

Overnight Oats

This is a quick and easy recipe for 2 to whip up the night before

It will ensure you have a tasty and filling breakfast in the morning

Ingredients

  • 100g oats
  • 20g chia seeds
  • 250g milk
  • 125g greek yoghurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 mashed banana
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • A sprinkling of cinnamon

Steps to make a tasty bowl of overnight oats

  1. Mash the banana with a fork until it is in a puree like form
  2. Add the banana to your container, along with the rest of the ingredients
  3. Stir thoroughly with a fork until everything is combined
  4. Place in the fridge to have in the morning
  5. Before eating, consider topping with crushed walnuts, dark chocolate chips, or blueberries!
